COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

How long does a restaurant website take?

A simple website may take around one to two weeks. Websites with ordering systems, dashboards, or integrations may take longer.

Can customers view my menu on their phones?

Yes. We can create a responsive menu experience for smartphones, tablets, and desktop screens.

Can you add online ordering and payments?

Yes, subject to project scope. Online ordering and payments need suitable backend functionality and, where applicable, a payment gateway integration.

Can I update my menu later?

Menu updates can be handled through developer support or a custom admin dashboard, depending on the solution selected.

Are domain and hosting included?

Only if included in your written quotation. Domain registration, hosting, renewals, and third-party services should be agreed before development begins.
