07 GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Learn more about our website redesign service before starting your project.

Ask us a question →
What is website redesign?

Website redesign involves improving an existing website's appearance, layout, usability, responsiveness, and, where required, its technical structure.

Will my existing website content be kept?

Existing content can usually be retained or reorganized. We will discuss what should remain, what needs improvement, and whether any content needs to be rewritten.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Responsive layouts are included in our proposed packages. We test important pages at different screen sizes to improve usability across devices.

How long does a redesign take?

The timeline depends on the number of pages, design complexity, required features, and availability of content. We will provide an estimated schedule after reviewing your project requirements.

Can you improve my website's SEO?

We can improve technical and on-page SEO foundations. Search rankings cannot be guaranteed, and we plan URL changes and redirects carefully to help protect existing visibility.

Can I request custom features?

Yes. Additional functionality can be discussed before the project starts. Features such as online payments, booking systems, customer accounts, and custom dashboards may require a separate quotation.
